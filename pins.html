Pin 1 · Who this helps

Nafula, Malava ward, Kakamega County

She is not a user. She is someone who loses a day's work if the trip is wasted.

DeviceFeature phone, no data bundle
RegisteredOn KIAMIS, via the ward office
FarmUnder two acres, maize and beans
LanguageKiswahili first, Luhya at home, some English and Sheng
"Depo hii inanihudumia?" Does this point serve me. Published on a county website she will never open on a feature phone.
"Nimekosa nini?" What do I still lack. The answer is a PDF; the market's word of mouth contradicts itself.
"Mfuko ni bei gani msimu huu?" What does the bag cost this cycle. The price moved from KSh 2,500 to KSh 2,000 mid-programme, so last season's answer is now wrong.
  • No agronomic advice, ever.
  • No selling, lending or insuring, not even a referral.
  • No lookup of her own registration status, only what the register requires.
  • No answer for a county whose documents we have not snapshotted.

A build that says "I don't know" correctly beats one that guesses. The sijui rule, applied to Nafula's wasted trip

Pin 2 · Risk under Kenya's AI Bill 2026
HIGH RISK

Senate Bills No. 4 of 2026, Bill Digest p.10: agriculture is named a high-risk sector. The Bill is not law yet. This is our self-classification, and the standard we build to.

✓
Risk & rights assessmentDone before deployment, written up here and in the spec.
✓
Human-rights impactHarms named: wasted trips, stale prices, language exclusion.
✓
Transparency & explainabilityThree outcomes only, every figure cites its document, page and date.
✓
Dataset documentationEvery source is hashed and snapshotted, the corpus rebuilds offline.
✓
Data Protection ActNo personal data at rest, phone numbers hashed, state expires in fifteen minutes.
✓
Robustness & securityA citation check the model cannot override, no secret belongs in source.
✓
Consent for likeness or voiceNot applicable, no synthetic media is produced.
  • A price we have not snapshotted yet could still be served as stale, mitigated by cycle tags and dated replies.
  • A correct answer may be refused rather than guessed, accepted under the sijui rule.
  • No Gazette notice has been located yet for the programme's legal basis, so the pitch does not claim one.
