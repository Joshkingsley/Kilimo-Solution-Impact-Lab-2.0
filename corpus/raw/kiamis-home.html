Kenya Integrated Agricultural Management Information System

KIAMIS is Kenya’s national digital public infrastructure for agriculture, providing a trusted, centralized farmer registry as a single source of truth. It enables government, development partners, and private sector players to securely design, deliver, and integrate interoperable agricultural services on a shared, standardized farmer data backbone.

Impact in focus

Transforming Agriculture

The Kenya Integrated Agricultural Management Information System (KIAMIS) serves as the national digital agriculture ecosystem, anchoring multiple sector platforms on a single, trusted farmer registry. By integrating farmer data, agripreneur programs, livestock interventions, and national projects, KIAMIS has transformed how agricultural services are planned, delivered, and monitored across all counties.

Impact at a Glance

  • ✅ Unified national farmer registry
  • ✅ Improved targeting of subsidies and interventions
  • ✅ Real-time monitoring of registration and approval workflows
  • ✅ Stronger coordination across counties and national government
  • ✅ Data-driven decision-making in the agriculture sector
