The contract

Three outcomes.

Cite

A figure, plus its source.

[Document, Page, Date], drawn from the retrieved chunk. If the number is not in the document, the reply is discarded.

Clarify

One question, asked once.

"Which county?" Held for fifteen minutes under a hashed key, then wiped. Never a second question.

Boundary

A fixed line, no hedging.

Out of scope, or not in the public documents. The same sentence every time, pointing to the ward office.

Never a fourth option.

Every reply lands in one of the three outcomes. Beside the phone, a judge panel shows the retrieved chunk, document, page and date behind each cited answer.

Senate Bills No. 4 of 2026, Bill Digest p.10, names agriculture a high-risk sector. The Bill is not law yet. This is our self-classification, and we build up to it, not around it.

Two residuals we accept: a correct answer may be refused rather than guessed; no Gazette notice has been located for the programme's legal basis, so we do not claim one.

Python ingestion, a TypeScript Worker, Cloudflare Vectorize, D1 and KV, Claude Haiku 4.5, Africa's Talking. No personal data at rest: phone numbers are hashed, state expires in fifteen minutes, logs never carry a number.
