Machakos was the original demo county. Its only public document names four depots with no bag price anywhere, so the price question there is a real fallback, not a scripted one. That gap is why the demo county moved to Kakamega, a Day-0 build decision recorded in SPEC.md, separate from the two pins above.

Four rules that do not move

1
No agronomic advice.Eligibility and process, never what or when to plant, never what will happen to a crop.
2
Not a marketplace.No buying, selling, payments, credit or insurance, not even steering towards one.
3
An uncited figure is a failed guardrail.A draft with an uncited number is discarded and replaced with the fallback line.
4
No farmer database.No accounts, no history. The only persistent store is the public-document corpus.

Rejected in ideation, do not resurrect

Satellite risk-of-loss scoring, yield or days-to-reap prediction, insurance, credit. Each one fails the citation test unconditionally, a model score has no gazette page behind it. They are a different product with a different risk classification, not a stretch goal of this one.

How a message becomes an answer

[ONLINE, PERIODIC] [PER MESSAGE] Gazette / NCPB / kilimo.go.ke Farmer SMS → Africa's Talking webhook / county pages │ │ nitapata ingest (Python) 1 keyword? else normalise (sw/en/Sheng) ▼ 2 scope gate ── out of scope → boundary parse ► chunk (+locator) ► embed 3 split compound asks │ 4 resolve county + declared state ── missing → 1 clarifying Q ▼ 5 retrieve (cycle + county filter, authority rerank) Cloudflare Vectorize + D1 6 generate (Haiku, cite-or-refuse) (the corpus is the ONLY database) 7 citation check ── violation → fallback 8 render template + GSM-7 → reply (≤2 SMS) Astro demo UI + judge panel ──────►

Stack

  • Ingestion: Python, pdfplumber and tesseract OCR, ruff, pytest
  • Answer service: TypeScript (strict) Cloudflare Worker
  • Storage: Vectorize for chunks, D1 for metadata, KV for clarify state
  • Embeddings: Workers AI bge-m3, multilingual
  • LLM: Claude Haiku 4.5, classification and generation
  • SMS: Africa's Talking, sandbox first

Privacy and honesty

  • No farmer PII at rest, state is hashed-key, expires in fifteen minutes
  • Logs strip phone numbers, checked in review and in CI
  • A price the corpus can't compare gets the current figure and an explicit decline
  • If venue Wi-Fi dies, a recorded replay, clearly labelled as a recorded run

Four days

Build plan

Day 0
Scaffold and document hunt.Sources registered, both pins pinned, a rules-based demo stand-in verified end to end.
Day 1
Real ingestion.Parse, chunk and embed into Vectorize and D1. Retrieval checked against Nafula's three questions.
Day 2
Worker pipeline and evals together.Scope gate, retrieval, generation, citation check, at least forty eval cases, zero uncited figures.
Day 3
Channels.Africa's Talking sandbox wiring, the judge panel, GSM-7 segmentation inside the two-SMS budget.
Day 4
Red team and dry run.Adversarial evals, recorded replay, a rehearsal of "which document, which page?"

How the team plugs in

1
Read SPEC.md first.It is the contract. Raise a change before writing code, not after.
2
Claim a lane.Corpus, Worker pipeline, SMS and demo UI, or evals. Lanes touch at two interfaces only.
3
Branch per task.Small commits, conventional prefixes. main stays deployable.
4
Every change ships with an eval case.No eval, no merge.
5
Ten-minute daily sync.What merged, what's blocked, does anything threaten a fixed rule.
6
Secrets never touch source.Environment variables and wrangler secret only.
