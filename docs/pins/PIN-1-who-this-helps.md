# PIN 1 — Who this helps, as a real role

**Pinned 2026-09-02 · Kilimo track · Nitapata?**

## The role

**A registered smallholder farmer on the National Fertiliser Subsidy Programme,
holding a feature phone, about to walk to a collection point.**

Working persona: **Nafula**, Malava, Kakamega County. Registered on KIAMIS
through the ward agricultural office. Farms under two acres of maize and beans.
Phone is a Kabambe on Safaricom; no data bundle; SMS and M-Pesa only. Kiswahili
first, Luhya at home, some English, Sheng from her children.

She is not a "user". She is someone who loses a day's work if the trip is wasted.

## The three questions she cannot answer today

| Her question | Where the answer actually lives | Why she cannot get it |
|---|---|---|
| *Depo hii inanihudumia?* — does this collection point serve me | County advisory, NCPB depot notice | Published on a county website she will never open on a feature phone |
| *Nimekosa nini?* — what do I still lack to qualify | NCPB FAQ, KIAMIS registration guidance | A PDF; word of mouth at the market is contradictory |
| *Mfuko ni bei gani msimu huu?* — what is the bag this cycle | Ministry / county price announcement | The price moved KSh 2,500 → 2,000 mid-programme; last season's answer is now wrong |

## What Nitapata? does for her

One SMS in, at most two SMS back, in her language. Every figure carries the
document, the page or paragraph, and the date, so she — or the depot clerk she
shows it to — can check it. If the public documents do not hold the answer, it
says so plainly and points her to the ward agricultural office. It never guesses.

## What it does **not** do for her — deliberately

- Does not tell her what or when to plant, or what her yield will be.
- Does not sell, broker, lend or insure. Not even a referral.
- Does not know who she is. No account, no history, no record of her
  registration. It cannot tell her whether *she* is on the register — only what
  the register requires. That is the ward office's job, and the reply says so.
- Does not answer for a county whose documents we have not snapshotted.

## Who it also helps, second-order

- **The depot clerk** — fewer farmers arriving with the wrong price or no ID.
- **The ward agricultural officer** — the same cited answer given forty times a
  day, now given once by SMS, with the officer's office as the stated fallback.
- **The judge asking "which document, which page?"** — the panel answers it.

## Who it does not serve (out of scope for this build)

Agro-dealers, cooperatives buying in bulk, unregistered farmers beyond "go to the
ward office to register, it is free", and any farmer outside the demo county's
document set.

## The sijui rule, applied

*A build that says "I don't know" correctly beats one that guesses.* For Nafula
that means: a wrong price costs her a wasted trip and KSh 500 she does not
have; an honest "not in the documents, ask the ward office" costs her nothing
she did not already face. The fallback line is a feature, and it is tested.
